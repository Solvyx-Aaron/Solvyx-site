Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Solvyx Financial LLC ("Solvyx", "we") provides bookkeeping for trades contractors. This policy explains what information this website and our tools collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form. If you send us a message, we receive your name, email, business type, and message. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms.
  • Call booking. Discovery calls are scheduled through Cal.com, which collects the details you enter when booking under its own privacy policy.
  • Client records. Financial information our bookkeeping clients share with us is handled under their engagement agreement and used only to provide the services they hired us for.

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to respond to you, schedule and hold calls, and provide services you engage us for. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it except with the service providers named above as needed to run those functions, or where required by law.

Google user data

Solvyx uses Google APIs (Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides) for internal business tools that access only Solvyx's own Google account. These tools do not request or access anyone else's Google data. Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to operate those internal tools, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your choices

We keep contact messages as long as needed to respond and follow up. You can ask us to delete or correct information we hold about you at any time.
